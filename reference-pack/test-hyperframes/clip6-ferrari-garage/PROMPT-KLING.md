# Prompt Kling per le immagini di riferimento (facoltativi)

Servono solo come riferimento di look per la scena Blender, non nel video. Kling IMAGE 3.0, 16:9. Li genera fede, se vuole, con i suoi crediti.

## 1. La rivelazione
```
Low-angle three-quarter front view, 35mm lens at f/2.8. A glossy red Ferrari 488 GTB sports car, Rosso Corsa paint with deep clear-coat reflections, sitting in the middle of a dark private garage, a grey canvas car cover sliding off its rear and pooling on the polished concrete floor. A small black quadcopter drone hovers above the rear, its white spotlight cutting through the haze. Dust particles drifting in a shaft of daylight from a half-open roller door behind the car. Cold fluorescent tubes on the ceiling just flickering on. Wet patches on the concrete reflecting the red bodywork. Moody high-contrast lighting, single saturated color, cinematic automotive commercial, photorealistic, subtle film grain. Aspect ratio 16:9.
```
Negative: `warped body panels, extra wheels, melted reflections, text, watermark`

## 2. Il garage prima (macchina coperta)
```
Wide establishing shot, 24mm lens, eye level near the floor. A dark private car garage, a sports car hidden under a grey fitted canvas cover in the center, a roller door half open at the back letting in a hard blade of warm daylight across the polished concrete floor. A small quadcopter drone flying in under the door, backlit, green and red LEDs glowing. Haze and dust visible in the light beam, tool cabinet and stacked tires in the shadows on the sides. Cinematic, high contrast, desaturated except for the warm light, photorealistic. Aspect ratio 16:9.
```
Negative: `cartoon, floating objects, warped walls, text, watermark`

## 3. Dettaglio eroe
```
Extreme close-up, 85mm macro, shallow depth of field. The side air intake and rear wheel of a red Ferrari 488 GTB, a soft strip of light slowly sweeping across the curved red clear-coat, carbon brake caliper visible through the black five-spoke rim. Dark garage background with blurred cold fluorescent lights. Luxury automotive commercial, photorealistic, crisp reflections. Aspect ratio 16:9.
```
Negative: `distorted rim, extra spokes, text, logo, watermark`

Variante da testare: cambiare solo la luce (`warm tungsten key, cool blue fill`) per vedere quale palette preferiamo.
