# Prompt Kling, pronti da copiare

Kling funziona meglio con i prompt in inglese. Formato 9:16 per tutto.

## A. Mascotte BELUGA (da fare per prima: crea l'Element)

**A1. Foglio modello (Kling Image 3.0, 16:9)**

```
Character turnaround sheet of a small white beluga whale puppet made of felt and wool, wearing a knitted red-and-cream striped sweater, round soft melon-shaped head, small black bead eyes, tiny smile. Front view, side profile, three-quarter view and back view in a row, plus three expressions: neutral, surprised, deadpan. Neutral light grey background, even studio light, same scale in every view, handmade stop-motion puppet, visible felt texture, no text.
```

Poi in Assets > Elements crea l'Element **Beluga** con la vista frontale come immagine principale e profilo, 3/4 e retro come immagini extra. Descrizione: "white felt beluga puppet, red-and-cream striped knitted sweater, bead eyes; ignore background".

## B. Clip 3, spot stop-motion (Kling Video 3.0, 9:16, 5 s, con l'Element)

```
Stop-motion animation. [@Beluga] sitting on a miniature rocky coastline with moss and tiny bushes. Overcast grey sky, soft diffuse daylight, fog over the sea behind. Locked-off static camera, medium shot, the character in the lower half of the frame. The beluga slowly blinks, turns its head to the camera and gives a tiny deadpan nod. Animated on twos, slight frame-to-frame jitter in the fabric fibres, visible felt texture, miniature scale, shallow depth of field, handcrafted like Aardman or Laika. No text, no logos.
```

Negative: CGI, smooth 3D render, glossy plastic, 2D cartoon, text, watermark, morphing, extra limbs, changing outfit.
