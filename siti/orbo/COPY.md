## Esploso (un'etichetta per pezzo, appare mentre il pezzo si stacca)

1. **Copper-ring touch surface**: tap, hold, swipe. Nothing to press.
2. **11 mm graphene driver**: deep bass without the boom.
3. **ORBO S1 chip**: adaptive noise cancelling, 48,000 times a second.
4. **Six microphones**: your voice stays clear, the street does not.
5. **Battery**: 8 hours in the bud, 40 with the case.

## Ricomposizione
